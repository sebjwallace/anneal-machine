Set up your own grid of columns, then design what happens over time. Each step of the timeline says which columns are active: a whole column (all its cells), or just the cells you pick. Hold keeps a column on at every step. Drag to paint; the faint columns are the step before. Play it back, or step through with the arrow keys.

This is a design pad: nothing runs on it yet. The design (and what's active at each step) is available to the page, ready for experiments such as transition memory to be wired in.
