# Regulators and heat: how a disturbance becomes a memory

A theory note, from conversation (13 November 2026), partly tested in experiments C59 and C60. It brings together three
things: associative memory (the engine's recall rule), heat (a misfit or a gap, and what it does to the bar), and a
picture of the whole system as regulators that disturb and settle one another. The craving for coffee is the running
example, because it is a gentle, everyday way to think about heat: like hunger, it rises, it is felt, it is acted on,
and it settles.

Related: [architecture and regulation](architecture-and-regulation.md) (regions, set points, connection types) and
[feelings as heat](feelings-as-heat.md) (trapped and outward heat). Visuals: `docs/visuals/craving.html`,
`heat-into-structure.html`, `c59-results.html`, `c60-results.html`.

## 1. The claim

Everything in the system is a regulator: a cell assembly, a column, a region, an organ in a body. Each has a resting
state it settles towards (its stasis). A disturbance pushes it away from that state, and the distance is heat. A
disturbed regulator sends out signals. The things that receive them are regulators too, and they respond by settling
themselves, which in turn changes what the first one receives. Together they fall back into a coherent, low-energy
state where energy flows in balance.

Memory sits inside this. A held disturbance (a craving) is heat that nothing yet explains. While it is held, the
system searches, acts, and learns, and when the disturbance is resolved, what happened between its rise and its fall
is kept as one memory. That memory is heat turned into structure: next time, the same disturbance is resolved
straight away, and the heat never builds.

## 2. Regulators everywhere

The owner's picture: an organ in the body is a regulator. Hunger, sickness and aching muscles are signals from organs
that have drifted from their resting state, messages for something else to take corrective measures. A region of the
brain is the same: it has a stasis, and deviation from it is heat that spreads to other parts of the system, which also
have to settle. The same is true inside the engine at the smallest scale: cells and assemblies settle into the
cheapest description (completion), and that settling is feedback that lowers energy.

So the principle that works inside a region (feedback in associative memory drops the energy into a low-energy state)
is taken up a level: regions, organs and whole systems settle each other in the same way in response to heat in one or
more of their parts.

Two existing words describe parts of this (they are named here only as pointers, not as the engine's terms):

- **Entrainment:** separate things with their own rhythms, interacting until they fall into step (pendulum clocks on
  one wall slowly syncing).
- **Allostasis:** staying stable by adjusting together, and in anticipation, rather than each part holding a fixed point
  alone.

Two consequences:

- **Set points come from outside a regulator.** A craving's rise is given to the brain by the body; the body's own set
  points were given by evolution. For the machine, the deepest set points have to be given by whoever builds it, and
  everything else can settle around them (as in the architecture note).
- **Resolve, don't mask.** A regulator can quieten a signal without fixing its cause. Heat that is covered rather than
  resolved stays trapped (feelings as heat, section 4). The machine has to settle the cause.

## 3. A want is held heat

A craving is a disturbance that is held: it stays active from the moment it rises until it is satisfied. While held,
nothing the machine knows explains it. That unexplained hold is heat.

In the engine's terms, the craving is held alongside where you are. It is present at every step, so it has no moment
of its own: it can't say *when* a memory happened (only where you are can), but it counts as one of the active things
inside any memory that holds it. This came out of C60 and turned out to matter (section 6).

The heat of a want is felt in stages (the owner's account):

- it rises gradually, perhaps below awareness at first (in the grassland);
- it grows until it is enough to reach the surface, and the valve opens (at the gate);
- once a way is found, part of it settles: there is a plan, and even some excitement, energy starting to flow;
- only arriving and being satisfied settles the rest (at the café, with the coffee).

## 4. Heat opens the valve; memory does the searching

When a held want can't be explained by anything that wakes at the normal bar (two active things inside one memory),
the heat lowers the bar. With the bar lowered, one active thing is enough to wake a memory, so memories wake out from
where you are and from what is wanted until they meet, and the bar goes back up (the moving bar, C44 and C51). No
planner and no route-finding algorithm are needed: it is the recall rule with the bar lowered.

In C59 the engine did this by itself: with coffee held as the end and the gate as the start, the bar dropped and the
sides met at the café: gate → fountain → café → coffee, a walk never taken as one.

## 5. Heat bounds a memory

The rise and fall of the heat is an arc: sunrise in the grassland, midday at the gate, sunset at the café. The owner's
point: **that arc is the boundary of the memory.** The opening of the gap (the heat rising) starts it, and the closing
of the gap (the want satisfied) ends it. Everything inside the arc is bound together as one memory, problem to
solution.

This answers two questions at once:

- **Where does a memory begin and end?** Experience has no natural edges otherwise. In C57, whole days given to the
  machine formed nothing bigger, because a day has no edges. Heat gives the edges.
- **How is the solution linked to the problem?** The usual approach in machine learning traces backwards from a reward
  to work out which steps led to it. Here nothing is traced: the memory already spans from the rise to the fall, and it
  holds the want, so cueing the want brings the whole of it back.

C60 tested this. Kept from where the heat rose to where it fell, the experience answered the next craving straight away
and lasted. Kept without the boundary (the whole stretch), it worked but brought back what came before and after. Every
walk kept regardless of heat: it worked at first, then ordinary walks pushed it out.

## 6. Heat into structure

The first time, the want costs a search: heat, a lowered bar, many memories woken. Once the arc is kept, the next time
the want is felt where you are, two active things (where you are and the want) fall inside one memory, so it wakes by
the plain recall rule. No valve, no search, almost no heat (C60: 7 memories woken, against a search the first time).
The heat of the first time has become the structure that keeps the heat down.

And between cravings, the memory is **primed, not woken**: where you are is one active thing inside it, and one isn't
enough. That is the owner's "under the surface". It isn't a strength or a weight; it is one versus two.

The want can even arise from the walk. Arriving at the fountain from the gate lights two things (the fountain now and
the gate just before), and what comes back depends on how distinctive the route is (C59): where the route leads many
ways, only the shared stretch; where you also walk past the café, the café comes to mind but not the coffee; where the
route is walked only for coffee, the coffee itself comes back.

## 7. What resolves a want: found by comparison

Everything inside the arc is bound to the want, the gate as much as the coffee. That doesn't make the gate a cause.
Coffee has resolved the craving at home, in the city and in the park; the gate was there once. At rest the engine
compares memories, and what they share becomes a part of its own (compare, don't average). Across many arcs, what they
share is the want and what ended it: the coffee. The places are details of each arc. So what resolves a want is found
by comparison over many experiences, not by tracing backwards. (Not yet shown: C60's three or so cravings per place
weren't enough for this to form. See section 9.)

## 8. Two kinds of memory, two rules

The experiments separated two ways of keeping:

- **General memory keeps what pays**, under the energy rule. Repetition pays: in C59 the walk was kept after four times.
  A single experience never pays, even with its heat counted in what is seen (C60): a memory costs something for every
  item in it, and one walk's heat costs less.
- **The store of experiences keeps at once**, under its own rule (C47). The arc decides what goes in and where it
  starts and ends: only heat-bounded experiences, so the store holds what mattered and isn't flooded by ordinary days.

So one-shot learning from a want lives in the store of experiences, bounded by heat, and general memory learns the
patterns that recur (including, eventually, the invariant: what resolves the want).

## 10. What it means for the engine

- **A held want sits alongside where you are, with no moment of its own.** It counts towards the recall rule but
  doesn't place a memory in time. (C60's copy of `think`; declared there.)
- **Experiences are bounded by heat.** The store keeps arcs, not everything.
- **The energy rule has no line for heat.** Energy is structure + flow + change; the heat of a held want, and the search
  it causes, aren't in it. C60 suggests general memory doesn't need it (experiences do the one-shot keeping), but the
  question stays open: should structure that removes heat be paid for by the heat it removes?
- **Nothing here is in the core.** It is on the branch and in experiment copies. The owner prefers to keep it there for
  now: bringing it into the core is its own piece of work.

## 11. Open questions and next experiments

- **Entrainment across regions:** several regulators, one or more disturbed, feeding back on each other until all
  settle. What does it look like, and what does it need beyond what a region already does? (Next visual.)
- **Coffee as the invariant:** more cravings in more places; does rest compare arcs and pull out "craving … coffee"?
- **Heat rising gradually:** the bar lowered in proportion to the heat, the want reaching the surface when it's enough.
- **Release:** the hold letting go once the want is explained, and the excitement once a plan is found.
- **Heat in the energy rule:** decide whether it belongs there.
