# Theories

Longer theory notes: ideas about how the machine could work, worked out in conversation, before (or while) they are
tested. Speculative unless a note says otherwise; each says which experiments support it. The settled core is in
[philosophy](../philosophy.md) and [principles](../principles.md); what has been tested is in the
[checkpoints](../checkpoints/README.md).

| Note | About |
|---|---|
| [Flow and heat](flow-and-heat.md) | The original premise (the owner's, recorded 15 November 2026): energy that flows and energy that can't; active assemblies as a living topology the energy flows through, with dead ends and loops where it is trapped; heat as the accidental energy, a symptom of misfit that spreads and changes activity first, then bonds; patches as heat baked into structure, repairs as the real fix; bonds as what cooling leaves behind; poles and arms, the pot, sinking by agreement and rising by disagreement, priming as friction warming the deep, warm meeting warm; letting go as evaporation; the machine as an annealer that takes in the world's heat, cools it inside, and reflects cooler structure back out. With how it meets C82-C85. |
| [Architecture and regulation](architecture-and-regulation.md) | Many regions, each the same engine with a few settings, wired by a few connection types; heat as distance from each region's own stasis, activity as how it gets back; the machine self-driven through what its regions leave unresolved. |
| [Regulators and heat](regulators-and-heat.md) | Everything is a regulator with a stasis; a disturbance is heat that spreads and is settled together. A held want (a craving) opens the valve, and its rise and fall bound a memory, so heat turns into structure. Partly tested in C59 and C60. |
| [Persistent assemblies](persistent-assemblies.md) | Settled assemblies stay active and change only when something arrives that doesn't fit them: held states without puppeteering, and event-driven computation. Untested. |
| [Feelings as heat](feelings-as-heat.md) | Human feelings read as heat and its flow: trapped heat as distress, patching as rationalisation, outward heat as curiosity, coherence as fulfilment; set points, resolving rather than masking, and the machine's purpose. |
| [Learnt logic](learnt-logic.md) | From the owner's other repository: cells do OR by adding up; segments make them OR of ANDs; inhibition learnt from the machine's own wrong predictions gives NOT; one rule with two signs of heat grows AND and AND NOT (the owner's NAND idea, AND NOT winning). Tested there in sketches 36-39, here C69-C72; with what it means for Anneal. |
| [Learnt logic: questions and answers](learnt-logic-qa.md) | Eight questions to the agent that did the learnt-logic work, its answers, and what they changed here: a candidate replacement for K (grow while misfit falls), why strengths were needed (random sampling), the two signs of heat being across time, and what picks the cell. |
| [Cells first](cells-first.md) | From bookkeeping to cells and segments (14 November 2026): columns keep memories apart across cells, segments within a cell; waking at two, completion, competition between whole memories; fitting by competition with re-settling where heat is left (in the core); composition by binding two cells per part; decomposition by the cut between fit and misfit; frames as a pattern, not a mechanism; work that follows what is active. With the evidence (C69-C78) and what is open. |
| [Learned computation](learned-computation.md) | The owner's step from transition memory to computation: the binding problem (what writes x, what reads it, what places 5 in "x − y"), answered by association with no registers or pointers: writing is bonding, reading is completion, an operator ties in and waits for what comes next, order comes from what was held against what arrives, and the answer is what the whole is associated with, primed before it lands. Computing is chaining learned associations. With the principle that composition and decomposition apply through transitions, as they did across a picture. Untested. |
