A craving as heat

A craving for coffee is held and unexplained: heat. The bar drops, memories wake from where you are and from the craving until they meet, and arriving at the café explains it. Drawn by hand to show the idea; see the story below.

What it shows

A craving read as heat. Wanting coffee is a held thing (the coffee cell stays lit) that nothing explains yet: no memory holds where you are and coffee together. That unexplained hold is heat. With where you are held at one end and the craving at the other, the heat lowers the bar, and memories wake from both ends until they meet: the path from the gate to the fountain, and the memory of having coffee at the café. The bar goes back up, leaving a plan. Walking it, the café and the craving finally fall inside one memory, and the craving is explained. The hold lets go and the heat is gone.

Why it works this way

It is the moving bar (see moving-bar.html) with a want as the far end. The café is found because it is what settles the gap: not low energy on its own, but low energy given the craving, because it is where the held thing fits. Heat falls in two stages, as the owner sees it: having a plan settles part of it (there is even some excitement, energy starting to flow), and only arriving resolves the rest.

What's simplified here

The rounds, memories and heat levels are drawn by hand. In experiment C59 the engine found this route by itself (the bar dropped, then the sides met at the café), but nothing in the engine yet measures a held craving as heat or lets go of it on arrival: those two parts are the owner's idea, not yet built.

Open questions
