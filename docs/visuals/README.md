# Visuals

Animations, interactive pieces and illustrations, used to agree how an idea works. Each one is a single HTML file
that opens in any browser with nothing else needed, and explains itself: under the visual is its story (what it shows,
why it works that way, what's simplified, open questions, and where it comes from). They are drawn by hand to show an
idea, not runs of the engine, unless they say otherwise.

| Visual | Shows | Belongs to |
|---|---|---|
| [moving-bar.html](moving-bar.html) | Heat opens the valve: start and end held, the bar drops from 2 to 1, memories wake from both ends, they meet, the bar goes back up and the route settles as it cools. | C44, C51, C54 (the moving bar) |
| [park-transitions.html](park-transitions.html) | Park walks as state transitions: landmarks as columns with now and was cells, a held context, remembered transitions that fit, and the most detailed fit predicting the next state. Holding a context picks the way at the fountain. | Change memory (`src/engine/change.js`, checkpoints 07 and 08) |
| [craving.html](craving.html) | A craving as heat: coffee held and unexplained, the bar drops, memories wake from where you are and from the craving until they meet at the café; arriving explains it and the hold lets go. | The owner's idea; C59 |
| [heat-into-structure.html](heat-into-structure.html) | The first craving needs a search and the satisfied walk is kept as one memory; the second time it comes straight back with no valve; with no craving it stays under the surface. With C59's results in the story. | C59 |
| [c59-results.html](c59-results.html) | **Engine results**, not a drawing: how many satisfied walks before the walk is kept (4), what the next craving then costs (no valve, 6 woken instead of 21), and what comes back with no craving in three parks. | C59 |
