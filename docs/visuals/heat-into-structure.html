Heat into structure: the first craving and the second

Three runs: the first time a craving needs a search, and the satisfied walk is kept as one memory; the second time it comes straight back; with no craving it stays under the surface. Drawn by hand; the experiment results are in the story below.

What it shows

Heat turning into structure. The first time you crave coffee at the gate, nothing holds the gate and coffee together, so the heat opens the valve and the machine searches until it finds the way through the café. When the craving is satisfied, the whole walk (gate, fountain, café, with coffee) is kept as one memory. The second time, the gate and the craving are two active things inside that one memory, so by the ordinary recall rule it comes straight back, whole, at the normal bar: no valve, no search, almost no heat. The heat of the first time has become the structure that keeps the heat down the next time.

With no craving, the walk stays under the surface: primed but asleep. It only wakes when its cue arrives.

Why it works this way

Nothing has to trace backwards from the reward, step by step, to work out which steps led to it (the usual approach in machine learning). The memory already spans the whole walk, and it is tied to the craving because the craving was held throughout. Cue the craving at the gate and the whole walk comes back. "Loosely there until the cue" isn't a strength or a weight: it is one active thing inside the memory (not enough) versus two (enough).

What the experiment found (C59)

The first craving: the engine found gate → fountain → café → coffee, never walked as one, with the bar dropping. Once the walk was kept, a craving at the gate was answered without the bar dropping, waking 6 memories instead of 21.

But one satisfied walk was not kept. The energy rule kept the walk only after it had been walked four times, because by then one memory was cheaper than describing each copy in pieces. Repetition paid; satisfaction didn't count. The engine's bill (structure + flow + change) has no line for the heat a craving costs, so a memory that would save that heat can't earn its keep from it. Putting heat in the bill is the owner's decision, and the obvious next experiment.

The second time is free of heat only if the two held ends (where you are and the craving) count together towards the recall rule's two. If each end counts only its own side, the bar still drops.

The "no craving" run is simplified here. In the engine, arriving at the fountain from the gate means two things are lit (the fountain now and the gate as "was"), and what comes back depends on how distinctive the route is. In a park where the route leads many ways, only "gate, fountain" comes back. Where you also walk past the café, the café comes to mind but not the coffee. Where the route is only ever walked for coffee, the coffee itself comes back: the craving arises from the walk.

Open questions

Should the heat a want costs be counted in energy, so that one satisfaction is enough to keep the walk? Should the two held ends always count together?
