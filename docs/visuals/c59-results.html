What it shows

The results of experiment C59, a run of the engine (the branch engine, abstract region over items), not a drawing. The question was whether a craving's heat turns into structure: whether a satisfied walk is kept as one memory, so that the next craving at the gate is answered without heat.

Part 1: after 1, 2 or 3 satisfied walks (gate, fountain, café, coffee) the walk is not kept, and a craving at the gate still opens the valve and searches (21 memories woken). After 4 it is kept as one memory, and the next craving is answered at the normal bar: no valve, 6 memories woken. Part 2: with no craving, arriving at the fountain from the gate (two things lit: the fountain now, the gate as "was") brings back as much as the route makes distinctive.

What it means

Heat does become structure, and once it has, the structure keeps the heat down. But the energy rule keeps the walk because it was repeated (one memory becomes cheaper than describing each copy in pieces), not because it ended a craving. The engine's bill is structure + flow + change, and it has no line for the heat a want costs, so a memory can't earn its keep by the heat it would save. In the owner's terms: the machine converts repetition into structure, but not yet heat.

The second time is free of heat only if the two held ends (where you are and the craving) count together towards the recall rule's two active things. Counted each on its own side, the bar still drops (14 woken).

The craving arising from the walk follows how distinctive the route is, with no strengths or weights: where the route leads many ways, only the shared stretch comes back; where you also walk past the café, the café comes to mind but not the coffee; where the route is walked only for coffee, the coffee itself comes back.

Choices in the experiment

Coffee is an item: the moment of having it, after the café (time runs along the walk). The craving is that item held as the end. Worlds of 200 days, rests every 50, seed 59; the flow window (100 pictures) decides how many repeats make a walk pay. "Ends together" is a new option in a copy of think.

Open questions

Should heat be in the bill, so that one satisfaction is enough? Should the two held ends always count together? What lets the held craving go once it is explained?
