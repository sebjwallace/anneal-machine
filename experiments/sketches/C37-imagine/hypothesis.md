# C37 · Imagination by heat and settling

Idea (owner): things are already broken into parts that fit together, so throwing the parts about (heat) and letting
them settle by the engine's own energy might make arrangements never seen: imagination, and with a goal, planning.

**Gravity.** With no input the cheapest page is an empty one, so settling needs a pull. Used here: the engine's own
reading. A page whose pieces sit in relations it knows reads as few big things (cheap); scattered pieces read as many.
A page's *saving* = lit pixels − the cost of the engine reading it. Pieces may only move, not change.

**World.** The moving world as still pictures (seeing only, as the engine test): bases table / frame / cart, tops
cap / peak / dots / hook. Never shown: frame + dots, cart + peak.

**Hoped for.** (1) Parts thrown apart fall back into the objects they came from. (2) Parts of a never-seen pair settle
into that new object. (3) With a piece held still (a goal), the rest settles into something that fits it.

Run: `node imagine.js` (env: RUNS, ONLY, HEAT=1 with T0/STEPS, GOALS=1).
