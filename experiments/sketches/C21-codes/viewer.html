Cells as coordinates

C21. The only columns are the primitives C20 learnt; composites (house, flag, ...) are not columns any more. Each column's cells form a grid of offsets. Floor 0 says where an instance is, measured from the centre of attention (the middle of the scene). The floors above hold assemblies: a composite is one cell per part, at that part's offset from the composite's own centre, so the same thing anywhere gives the same assembly. A composite of composites takes a sample of 3 cells from each child. Scenes are explained with the best (cheapest) explanation, not just the greedy one.
