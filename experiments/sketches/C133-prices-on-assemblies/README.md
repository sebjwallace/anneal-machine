# C133 · Prices on assemblies

Distillation D-03, V3. Cost as surprise read from a central table of uses is replaced by each thing's own evidence: a
piece's uses counted on its own state, a letter's on its own population, and the whole from the engine's own clock (the
moments lived, one a letter). On a neutral yardstick, which costs each unit by how often it occurs in the whole book's
description, energy is within half a percent of the table's on both books. Memory is smaller. Reading is two points
better on *Buster Brown* and three worse on *Alice*. The table, the total and the count of kinds are gone.
