C80 · Reading from letters, one region. Written 14 November 2026 after smoke runs on development seed 1, before any run
on test seeds 101-105. Settings in run.js: 24 words (8 subjects, 6 verbs, 10 objects, several sharing beginnings),
400 random three-word sentences read once as one stream, with spaces and without; the cells engine with 32 cells per
column and context of two steps, against one cell per letter. One change was made while looking at dev seed 1, and is
declared: boundaries are guessed where the number of letters predicted (the breadth) peaks, as well as where a burst
happens; bursts alone stopped marking boundaries once every possible first letter was predicted.

Seen on dev seed 1 (last quarter): cells inside words right 99 / 96%, sharp 76 / 66% (with / without spaces); one cell per
letter sharp 38 / 30%; without spaces, boundaries where the breadth peaks precision 76%, recall 85% (one cell per letter
51 / 62%); from surprise alone, recall 18%.

Predictions (cells arm, last quarter, unless said):
  P1  Inside words, the next letter is predicted right >= 95%, with and without spaces.
  P2  Sharper than one cell per letter by >= 25 points, with and without spaces.
  P3  Without spaces, boundaries where the breadth peaks: precision >= 70%, recall >= 80%, and both >= 15 points above
      one cell per letter.

  P5  The breadth at first letters is >= 2.5 times the breadth inside words.
  P6  Without spaces, boundary precision rises by >= 10 points from the first quarter to the last.
