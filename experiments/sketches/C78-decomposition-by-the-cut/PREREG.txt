C78 · Decomposition by the cut. Written 14 November 2026 after smoke runs on development seed 1, before any run on test
seeds 101-105. Settings in run.js: 100 columns; parts of 6 columns; families of a house (wall + roof) and a shed (the
same wall + another roof); the house stored whole, then the shed arrives and lights whole columns; the cut read from
cells' states; decomposition and rebinding by two sampled cells per part.

Arms: cut=top (the cells at the top level of support are the memory that fits; C74) or cut=all (every cell with
support >= 2); read=plain or read=whole (binding segments fire only at the top active share among binding segments
firing that round: competition between whole memories).
Runs: cut=top at 16 and 64 cells per column, M = 10, 50, 200 families. cut=all (slow: its parts grow huge) at 64 cells
per column, M = 10 and 50, and 16 cells per column, M = 10.

Seen on dev seed 1: cut=top found the right wall, roof and new roof in 100% of families everywhere; 114 links against
264 for both wholes; read=plain: the other roof came back every time; read=whole: each roof brought back its own whole,
clean 100 / 86 / 65% (M = 10 / 50 / 200) at 16 cells and 94% at 64 cells, M = 200; from the wall alone both roofs
came back every time. cut=all: the cut was right in about 8% of families at M = 50.
