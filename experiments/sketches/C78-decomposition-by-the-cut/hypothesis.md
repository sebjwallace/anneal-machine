# C78 · Decomposition by the cut: hypothesis

Decomposition in the engine is bookkeeping: at rest, every pair of parts is compared at every shift, and every recent
picture is refitted for every trial. The proposal (14 November 2026, agreed with the owner) is to do it with cells:

1. A house is stored. A shed arrives, sharing the house's wall. Its columns light all their cells, so the house's wall
   cells see each other active and the house partly wakes. The comparison happens by itself (Sagan's "intercompares").
2. Every cell's state draws the cut: lit and predicted (the wall fits), predicted but not lit (the house's roof), lit but
   predicted by nothing (the shed's roof).
3. Decompose along the cut: the fitting cells become their own assembly on the same cells (the abstraction, really
   shared); the house's roof becomes its own; the shed's roof gets fresh cells; each whole is rebound by two sampled
   cells per part (C76). Kept only if it pays, counted in links.

**Hypothesis:** read through the top level of support (C74), the cut finds the right parts; the result costs fewer links
than storing both wholes; and with competition between whole memories, either roof brings back its own whole and not
the other. Predictions in [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt).
