# C62 · A reading world with known rules

The first text world, chosen inside the Zorro circle: what the branch engine already does (the abstract region over
items, comparing at rest) applied to letters. A small made-up language (11 words, one sentence pattern: the/a, cat/dog/
fox/hen, sat/ran/hid, on/by, the/a, cat/dog/fox/hen) is read through an 8-letter window that lands at a random place
each look. Question: does comparing what it has read find the words as parts, with nobody telling it what a word is?
Two conditions: spaces kept, and spaces removed (the words run together). Measured: the assemblies kept (are they
words?), and how it cuts new text (do its cuts fall between words?) against random cuts at the same rate. Givens are
declared in run.js (G1-G4). Run: `node run.js` (out.txt).
