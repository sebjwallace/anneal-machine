# C59 · A craving: heat that becomes structure

Owner's idea (13 November 2026). A craving (coffee) is a held thing that nothing explains yet: heat. Held at one end,
with where you are held at the other, the heat lowers the bar and memories wake from both ends until they meet (the
moving bar, C44/C51): gate → fountain → café → coffee. Walking it and having the coffee is satisfaction, and the whole
walk is kept as one assembly. Next time, the gate and the craving are **two active things inside one memory**, so it
comes straight back at the normal bar: no valve, no search. Heat has become structure. And with no craving, the walk
stays under the surface; arriving at the fountain from the gate (now and was: two things) could even suggest the
coffee, but only where that route is distinctive to coffee (otherwise the tie rule keeps it quiet).

Questions:
1. First time: does `think(gate, coffee)` find the way through the café, with the bar dropping (heat)?
2. Is the lived walk kept as one assembly by the energy rule alone, and does it survive rest and later days?
3. Second time: does it come back without the bar dropping, and with how much less search?
4. No craving: does "gate, then fountain" suggest coffee where that route is distinctive, and stay quiet where it isn't?

Setup: the C56 branch engine, abstract region over items (`o.atoms`), time along x. Coffee is an item: the moment of
having coffee, which follows the café. Nothing in the engine is changed. One choice is tested both ways (see run.js):
whether the two held ends count **together** towards the recall rule's two (`joint`), or each side counts on its own
(the branch's `think`, unchanged).
Run: `node run.js`.
