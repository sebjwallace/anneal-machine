# C81 · Levels from letters

Do composition and abstraction emerge above letters in context? Words held as assemblies, shared parts cut out of them,
sentences bound, all on the cells engine, from a stream of letters with no spaces. Hypothesis, predictions, findings:
[hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt), [findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (a few seconds; `SEEDS=1` is the development seed).

**Audit:** read against the contracts afterwards; recognition, parts and the boundary rule were done by the experiment's code (bookkeeping), nothing is kept by the energy rule, and sentences were grouped and chosen using the truth, so the sentence results can't be claimed. See findings.md.

**In short (what survives the audit):** with the right boundaries, every level forms: one chain of cells per word, the true shared
beginnings as parts, every sentence bound and its object recalled from subject and verb. Two regions are needed, one to
mark boundaries and one to hold words. With the regions' own boundaries (74% right) the levels form, but noisily: half
the words recognised exactly, most parts fragments, sentences bound only where every word was cut exactly.
