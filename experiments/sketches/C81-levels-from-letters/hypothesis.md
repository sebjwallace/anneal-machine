# C81 · Levels from letters: hypothesis

C80's region learnt letters in context and showed where words begin, but nothing was composed or abstracted: words
existed only as chains of context cells, different after every different preceding word. **Hypothesis:** levels emerge
from cells alone if, at the region's own boundaries, a word is read from a fixed boundary context, so the same word gets
the same cells. Then:
- words are held as assemblies (in the code, every run is stored on first sight and "recognised" when it recurs: not yet kept by the energy rule; see the audit in findings.md);
- a word whose cells already belong to another stored word yields the shared run as a part (an abstraction, such as
  "ca" in "cat" and "car");
- a sentence's three words are bound by two cells each, and from two of its words the third comes back.
