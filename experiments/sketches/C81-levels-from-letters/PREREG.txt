C81 · Levels from letters. Written 14 November 2026 after smoke runs on development seed 1, before any run on test
seeds 101-105. Settings in run.js: C80's stream without spaces (24 words, 400 three-word sentences, read once); the cells
engine, 32 cells per column, context of two steps.

Changes made while looking at dev seed 1, declared:
  1. The region's boundary guess is the peak of breadth, with one letter of look-ahead (the region predicts the next
     letter without learning, then decides); a guess on every rise was 31% right.
  2. The engine now keeps sequence segments ('seq', predicting the next input from what came before) apart from
     assembly segments ('word', 'part', 'bind', completing what is active now). Without it, stored words predicted all
     their letters at once and spoiled the boundaries. The engine's tests still pass.
  3. A two-region arm was added: region 1 never lets go and marks boundaries; region 2 lets go at region 1's boundaries
     and holds the words, parts and sentences. Letting go in the region that marks boundaries spoiled them (46% right).
  4. The measure of shared parts was widened: a part is a shared beginning (two or more real words begin with it), a
     whole word found inside a longer run, or neither. Measuring code only.
