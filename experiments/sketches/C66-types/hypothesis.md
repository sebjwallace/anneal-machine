# C66 · Types from what surrounds words

C65 found constants from a handful of sentences, but no types: sun, sea and fire share no letters, so a type for them
can only come from what surrounds them. Question: if a second region receives, for each word read, its surroundings with
the word's own place marked ("the here warms the sea"), does the same engine, comparing at rest, find shared
surroundings, and do the words explained by the same shared surrounding form the right groups? A small world with four
known types (heat sources, things warmed, water, growing things) and four frames; each sentence seen once, one rest;
4 to 24 sentences. Givens in run.js (G1-G3; G3, the wiring of the second region, is the big one). Run: `node run.js`.
