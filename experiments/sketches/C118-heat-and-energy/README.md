# C118 · Heat and energy

C114's differentiation with the project's two missing principles. With heat on, only what reading leaves unexplained
is compared and stored. With energy on, a piece derived by a comparison is kept only if it pays. Together they nearly
double coverage (to 66 to 77%) and words read whole (to 23 to 28%). They bring cuts across the whole text to 44 to 48%
at word boundaries (chance about 25%) with the plain cheapest reading, and halve the pieces held. The system cools over
the book: the share of each sentence still unexplained falls from about 80% to about 25%. The clean share of pieces
rises by 9 to 13 points on *Buster Brown* but only 3 on *Alice*. Heat alone makes pieces less clean, because the hot
gaps it stores as raw experience are fragments and reading uses them. The next question is whether raw experience
should be compared but not read with.
