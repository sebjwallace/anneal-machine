# C88 · Lowering the bar (primitive): hypothesis

Written 15 November 2026, after C87 and the owner's description of the aha ([flow and
heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md), section 7): a piece that settles on the surface makes the deep lower
its bar, activity spreads from the piece's two ends, and where the fronts meet the dots connect, the deep lights up and
the piece slots in. C87 found the core's deep already lights everything connected from any cue, so there is no near or
far, and no aha.

**The change (model.md):** the core's own settling rounds, let through one at a time from each end. The bar starts high
(no spreading) and drops by one round per step, until the fronts meet or stop.

**Primitive, on purpose (the owner's):** a real deep is probably already primed to welcome new information and would
need its bar lowered far less. Here it starts from nothing.

**Hypothesis:**
- connected links meet and unconnected ones don't, as in C87, but now locally: at the meeting, only a little of the
  deep is lit;
- a link with more support meets sooner, or on more at once: "cat mammal" (cats walk like three mammals) against
  "fish mammal" (fish swim like one), so fit is graded, near or far, snug or loose.

The world gains a horse and makes the dog a mammal, so that the two links differ in support.
