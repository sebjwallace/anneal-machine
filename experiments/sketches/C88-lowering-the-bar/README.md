# C88 · Lowering the bar (primitive)

The owner's aha: the deep lowers its bar one round of settling at a time from each end of a new link, until the fronts
meet ([model.md](model.md)). Primitive on purpose: a real deep is probably primed and would need far less. Hypothesis,
predictions, findings: [hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt), [findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (under a second; `SEEDS=1` is the development seed).

**In short:** lowering the bar gives the deep locality (at the meeting each end has lit about one thing, against eight
when settling runs to the end), and connected links meet while unconnected ones never do. "Cat mammal" never meets
later than "fish mammal", but the size of the aha is not yet graded (3 of 5 seeds).
