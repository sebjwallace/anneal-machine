# C65 · Frames from a handful of sentences

The owner's point: statistical learning needs a great deal of text because it estimates from counts; comparison sees
what is shared directly, from two examples, so constants, frames and the groups in their variables should appear from a
handful of sentences after one rest. Tested with 2 to 12 sentences about energy flowing from one thing to another
("the sun warms the sea"), words given as items, each sentence seen once, then one rest. Two ways of keeping the
sentences: by energy (the engine as is) and held at once as experiences, so they take part in rest. Measured: the
constants and frames found, how much the rest compressed, and whether never-seen sentences are explained by what was
found. Givens in run.js (G1-G3). Run: `node run.js` (out.txt).
