# C60 · Heat bounds a memory

Owner's idea (13 November 2026), following C59. A craving (or hunger: a signal from the body, a set point given from
outside) rises somewhere before the gate, in the grassland, and is held until it is satisfied at the café. While held,
it is broadcast: it is part of what the region receives at every step, and nothing known explains it, so it is heat.
The memory that matters runs from where the heat rose to where it fell: the rise and fall of heat is the boundary of
the memory (its start and its end), and everything inside that arc is bound together, problem to solution. No tracing
backwards from the reward.

C59 found that one satisfied walk isn't kept by the energy rule (it was kept only after four). Questions:
1. Does broadcasting the craving (heat in what is seen) make one satisfied walk worth keeping by energy alone?
2. If the arc is kept at once as an experience (the store of experiences, its own rule, C47), does the second craving
   come back without the valve opening, and does it last?
3. Does the boundary matter? Compare keeping the arc, keeping the whole stretch (before the heat rose and after it
   fell), and keeping every walk as an experience.
4. Across cravings satisfied in different places (home, city, park), does what resolves the craving (coffee) separate
   from the places?
