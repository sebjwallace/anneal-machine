## What it shows
- **Energy alone never keeps a one-off, even with the heat in what is seen.** A memory costs a placement per item; a
  single picture's unexplained heat costs less. In this currency, structure only pays for what recurs. So the heat of a
  craving can't buy its memory through the energy rule; one-shot memory needs the store of experiences (C47 showed the
  same for experiences in general).
- **The arc is the right boundary for an experience.** Kept from where the heat rose to where it fell, the experience
  answers the next craving at the normal bar, with no valve, and lasts: the store only holds arcs (heat decides what is
  worth keeping as an experience), so nothing pushes it out. Keeping every walk works at first but the arc is soon
  pushed out by ordinary days. Keeping the whole stretch works, but brings back what came before and after.
- **The craving belongs with where you are, not ahead of it.** Held alongside (no "when" of its own), it counts as one of
  the two active things that wake a memory, and where you are places it in time. That is what makes the second time
  free of heat, without the "ends count together" choice from C59.
- **Not yet: coffee separating out as what resolves the craving.** With 3 cravings at home, 3 in the city and 1 in the
  park, no part holding the craving and coffee apart from any place formed. Replay at rest didn't make them recur
  enough. The owner's point (coffee is the invariant because it has resolved the craving in many places) needs more
  cravings, or comparison across experiences at rest.
