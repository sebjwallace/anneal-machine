# C61 · Looking for lost keys

Owner's thought experiment (13 November 2026). Searching the house for keys, memory stops you looking again where you
have already looked. But the engine has no negative associations ("kitchen, no keys"). Does it fall out of what we
have: pricing a claim against evidence, where a claim about a place the machine has seen costs if it's wrong, and a
claim about a place it hasn't looked at costs nothing (hidden)? The owner's condition: rooms must become "seen" only by
the machine's own looking. Nobody marks them. Every given is declared in run.js (G1-G5), separately from what the
machine does (M1-M4), and the claim is only about the latter. A puppet control shows what marking rooms by hand would
have "shown". Run: `node run.js` (out.txt).
