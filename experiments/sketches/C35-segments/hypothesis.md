# C35 · Dendritic segments: hypothesis

The owner's earlier finding: giving each cell separate segments, one per assembly it belongs to, each firing on a fuzzy AND of its members, raised capacity and kept similar patterns apart. Plain cells pool all their connections, so a cell acts as an OR.

Prediction:
1. Today's completion, which counts each assembly's evidence separately and then lets them compete, is the segments idea in bookkeeping form. A segment network with competition should give identical answers.
2. Pooled cells should confuse things as more are stored, while segments shouldn't.
3. Segments that accept a member one step out of place, at a price on the bill, might recall from displaced cues better.
