# C90 · Warm meets warm

A confirmation of C89's after-the-fact observation, predicted first, on ten fresh seeds and two new animals, with C89's
engine and test unchanged. Hypothesis, predictions, findings: [hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt),
[findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (seeds 201-210; a second or two).

**In short:** confirmed. After a walking context, "cat mammal" and "cow mammal" met on 9 cells against about 4, in 10 of
10 seeds each; the swimmers, after swimming, met bigger or sooner in 8 and 9 of 10. One prediction failed on a mislabel:
the walking context is about mammals, so it also helped the swimmers' "mammal" end. Priming made no unconnected link
meet.
