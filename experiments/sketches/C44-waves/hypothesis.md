# C44 · Pushing out from both ends (owner's propagation waves)

C41–C43: two walks that share a junction never join, because the junction is never lit: one clue can't bring a walk
back, so nothing reaches the place where they meet. Owner's idea: when thinking, a lit state pushes activity out to
what it's connected to, from the start and from the end. Where the two pushes land on the same state, it lights up
for real, and the ordinary recall rule then brings each half back: a join. Pushes go up through bigger memories (longer
walks) as well as along, so longer memories carry the push further in one go (the aeroplane), and coming back down only
happens through a join; no join, no way down (a gap). No pixels; states are items. Run: `node waves.js` (0.2 s).
