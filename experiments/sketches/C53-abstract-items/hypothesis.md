and joins may belong in the abstract region, whose input is what the region below recognised. Such a region's parts
are its incoming items (its columns), and naming one thing costs picking one of all item-and-place possibilities.

- `o.atoms`: the region's parts are its items; a walk is remembered as one new assembly of its items (as a picture is
  remembered as one new part), and assemblies are never fused into plain parts.
