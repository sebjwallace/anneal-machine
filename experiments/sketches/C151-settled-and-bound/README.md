# C151 · Settled and bound

C150's learning in the circuit, with judging, choosing and binding moved into the circuit. Cooling is a cell switched
on by an agreement gate; a misfit stops the lane's clock; "Settled" is a gate needing every cell of the working cooled;
a fixed rule binds a settled lane's step to "+" in a long-term store, and what is learned is whatever the store gives
(the structure bound most often wins). All 20 seeds learned the right step and added 4 to 8 digit numbers 100% right.
Without carries, two explanations tie for ever and the store gives nothing, rather than guessing as C150's order did.
Proposing candidates, and declaring the working as heat, remain the experiment's.
