# C182 · Convergence and the map

The design settled with the owner (docs/theories/convergence-and-the-map.md), built with the map, residue heat and
convergence as structure in the substrate, and a small fixed rule reading them. The part most heated relative to what
has converged on it gives way, one per attempt. Alternatives are tried nearest first, skipping slimy rocks (residue in
context) and ways of working already on the map. No change rides along, and worse brings the best back. No churn, and
times one digit took 12 runs (200 of 200). The second row stuck one step short every time (0 of 200), because the two
changes it needed had been marked as dead ends when first tried in the wrong company. The residue's context, the worked
example, was too coarse; it should be the situation, including the rest of the way of working.
