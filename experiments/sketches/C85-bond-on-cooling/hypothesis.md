# C85 · Bond on cooling: hypothesis

Written 15 November 2026, after C84 and the owner's [flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md) premise. C84
found that the core never leaves a one-off unexplained. Any two of its columns wake an old memory, which then claims
the rest of itself, and the cut and the tie rebuild the one-off from fragments. Heat that should have passed through is
baked into structure, as a patch: "something held that isn't there" never falls, and with shared columns everything
chains to everything.

The premise says bonds are what cooling leaves behind, not what heating makes, and that not every heat should become
structure. The old bill already said how to tell: a memory used to explain an input is worth it only if what it
explains outweighs what it claims that isn't there, both priced at 1 (RM-9).

**The changes (arms, each on a copy of C84's engine):**
- **pays:** after settling, each memory woken (read from the cells, as the cut reads them) is weighed. If it claims more
  cells that aren't there than it explains, it takes no part in the moment. Its seen cells win nothing, so what it would
  have explained stays new: a loose assembly on fresh cells, and nothing of the old memory is cut or tied.
- **pays-squeeze:** and C82's loosening, and C84's squeeze (a full column clears its least-backed cell), so the heat of
  one-offs can flow out as their loose bonds are cleared.
- **cooling:** and a whole seen again fills in one missing link per cell (C84's densify). A bond freezes by returning
  and fitting, and frozen bonds are the last to be cleared.

**Hypothesis:** in the cooling arm, structure stays flat, "something held that isn't there" falls far below the core's,
recurring objects keep their recall, and the one-offs' heat is left honestly unexplained rather than patched.

**A known cost, seen in the core's tests before the run:** the bill forbids recall from a small cue. A quarter of the
square explains 4 cells and claims 12, so it no longer brings back the whole (five of the core's tests fail in every
pays arm). In a stream the machine can't tell "not there" from "not yet seen".
