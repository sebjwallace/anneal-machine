# C89 · Priming from the surface

The owner's priming: what turns at the surface warms the deep beneath it, and a warm piece meets a warm, welcoming
place ([model.md](model.md)). C88's lowering bar, with a context run first. Hypothesis, predictions, findings:
[hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt), [findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (under a second; `SEEDS=1` is the development seed).

**In short:** a matching context brings the meeting sooner where there is room ("fish mammal" after swimming, 3 of 5
seeds) or bigger where there isn't ("cat mammal" after walking met on twice as many cells, seen after the fact). Other
contexts change nothing, and priming never makes an unconnected link meet. The preregistered "sooner in 4 of 5" fails.
