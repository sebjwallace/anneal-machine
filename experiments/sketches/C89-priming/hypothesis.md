# C89 · Priming from the surface: hypothesis

Written 15 November 2026, from the owner's picture ([flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md), section
7): as the poles turn at the surface, friction warms the deep beneath them, priming it where a new piece is likely to
land. A warm piece meets a warm, welcoming place, and the residual heat lets them bond. C88 lowered the bar from cold,
evenly, and got locality but no graded fit.

**The change (model.md):** the deep's winners during a short context stay active as warm cells. Warmth helps a deep
cell wake alongside the new piece, never by itself; a warm cell the front reaches joins it and counts as a meeting
point.

**Hypothesis:** a context that matches the link (walking before "cat mammal", swimming before "fish mammal") brings the
meeting sooner; a mismatched or unrelated context doesn't; and priming never makes an unconnected link meet.
