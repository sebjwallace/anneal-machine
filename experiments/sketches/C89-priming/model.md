# C89 · Draft model: priming from the surface (written before the code)

**For:** the owner's priming ([flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md), section 7): the poles that turn
at the surface warm the deep beneath them, so the deep is ready where a new piece is likely to land; a warm piece meets
a warm, welcoming place.

**Warmth, in cells, without a number:** the deep's cells that won their columns in the recent moments of the surface
(the poles that turned) stay active, as lingering activity. They are not seen now, so, like anything brought back, they
count towards firing but are never evidence in competition (PRN-5). That lowers the bar locally: a deep cell with one
warm neighbour and one from the new piece can wake where, cold, it would need two from the piece.

```
the deep (the core) has learnt its world
context: a few surface moments; warm = the deep's winners in them (gated: the deep learns nothing from them)
a new link A B arrives (still warm itself: its own cells are the cue)
    baseline: what the warm cells bring back on their own (their own closure); this is not counted as meeting
    front A: settling from A with the warm cells active, one round at a time (C88's lowering bar)
             counted: cells brought back beyond A's columns and beyond the warm cells' own closure
    front B: the same from B
    the fronts meet when they share a counted cell: the step, and what they share, as in C88
```

**Given by hand, declared:** the context, and how long warmth lingers (the whole context, then gone: no fading); the
test is run by the experiment, as in C87 and C88. Still primitive.
