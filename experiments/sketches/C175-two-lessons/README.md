# C175 · Two lessons (a taste)

Gradual learning, first rung. Lesson 1, adding with carrying, is learned from scratch by doing and checking. Lesson 2,
times one digit, is learned from scratch (1,112 tries) and with lesson 1's pieces offered first. With all of lesson 1's
equal ways of working offered, it took 180 tries; with the right one alone, 2. All arms learned the same way of working,
200 of 200. But only 1 of lesson 1's 6 equals leads to times one digit: adding couldn't tell which read is the carry,
and times one digit needs to know. The two lessons look alike on paper, so the saving was expected (said before the run).
