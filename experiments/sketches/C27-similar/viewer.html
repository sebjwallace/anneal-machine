Same, or similar?

The machine watched two-object demonstrations, as pictures only, in rooms whose walls are bricks. It learnt what happens next to each object from its surroundings, looking further out only when nearer didn't settle it. Then, from the first two pictures of a new start, it imagines the rest with no input. Two ways of using memory: exact (C26) only trusts situations seen exactly like this; similar (C27) trusts exact first, but when a situation is new it recalls the most similar one, where positions that are close count as alike. Objects in red are not where the truth has them.
