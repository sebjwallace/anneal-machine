**Lowering the bar under heat gives exactly C44's results**: A and O → A → J → O; the long park → the full 9-place
route; with a fork and only short walks → undecided; with the longer walk in memory → the walked way; no connection →
no way through. Longer memories again mean less work (18 assemblies woke instead of 33).

**So pushing is not a separate mechanism.** It is the ordinary recall rule with its bar lowered while a gap is open:
at bar 1, one active state wakes every assembly it belongs to, which is what "pushing" did. When the gap closes the bar
goes back to 2 and only states active from both sides settle.

**Bar not raised again:** what's left active is everything woken (12 states, side paths included), not the 9-place route.

**Note.** Even a walk it has stored needs the bar lowered once here, because start and end are held separately and
their timing relative to each other isn't known until an assembly links them.

**Result.** The owner's picture holds: an open gap (heat) lowers the bar, more assemblies wake, the gap closes, the bar
goes back up and the route settles. One rule with a moving bar instead of a separate pushing procedure. Engine unchanged.
