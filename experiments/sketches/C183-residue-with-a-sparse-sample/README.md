# C183 · Residue with a sparse sample

C182 with residue heat bound to the part at fault and a sparse random sample (2 elements) of its situation, felt only
where that sample is present, and barring a change only when it outweighs the alternative's convergence. That's the
owner's idea of residue generalising by convergence, with a sparse sample keeping it loose. The second row was learned
right in 2 of 3 seeds (154 and 196 runs, 200 of 200), and the third stuck. Times one digit stayed easy (11 or 12 runs).
Success came when a later worked example let one half of a needed pair show an improvement alone. The pair problem
remains.
