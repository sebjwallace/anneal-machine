# C63 · Words as what fills the slots

Follows C62, which found that energy keeps whatever recurs, so whole words lose out to chunks across words and pieces
shared between words, and more reading finds fewer words. A word varies independently of its neighbours; in the
engine's terms that is a **kind**: one form with a slot. The branch engine already forms kinds over items. Question: do
the words stand out as what fills the slots, and do the slots group words by the part they play? Same world, engine and
givens as C62; nothing changed or added. Run: `node run.js` (out.txt).
