# C160 · Keeping what episodes share (a taste): hypothesis

Follows [C159](../C159-context-picks-the-frame/findings.md), a taste in which each of the two frames (reading,
holding) was remembered with what was in view when it worked. Whenever context picked a frame it picked the right one
(0 wrong of 320), but most sums recalled nothing. Episodes of the same frame disagree on incidental digits, two digits
in one place are rivals, and the episodes tie.

**The idea, a taste by agreement with the owner:** keep what a frame's episodes share. When a frame settles a sum, the
binding rule binds it with that view, as before, and also with **the overlap of that view and each view the frame was
bound with before** (an empty overlap binds nothing). After two additions the overlap is the "+" under the starting
column and the 0 carried in; the digits drop out. Nothing says which places matter. The overlap is whatever stayed the
same. The abstraction is leaner than any episode, so the engine's preference for the leaner of overlapping structures
should now work for it, not against.

**Given, declared:** as C159 (the two frames as already learned, the signs' meanings, binding the start view), plus the
intersecting, done by the binding rule, not by the engine's own dynamics. Same sums and seeds as C159, with C159's
episodes-only binding run alongside for comparison.

## Predictions (fixed before running)

1. **With overlaps, nearly every sum recalls a frame, and the right one:** the reading frame for "+" and "−", the
   holding frame for both kinds of multiplication. All four kinds mostly right, far above C159's 7 to 24%.
2. **The risk named beforehand:** the reading frame also gets a near-empty abstraction (an addition and a subtraction
   share only the 0 carried in), which matches any page. On a multiplication, the holding frame's abstraction (which
   has the "×") should still win, because more of it is in view. If it doesn't, multiplications will recall the reading
   frame and fail.
