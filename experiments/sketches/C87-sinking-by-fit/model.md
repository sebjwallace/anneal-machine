# C87 · Draft model: sinking by fit (written before the code)

**For:** the [flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md) rule "sink what fits, hold up what conflicts, let
go of what never settles", on C86's two volumes.

**The idea, in cells:** a new link between two things, A and B, fits the slow structure when that structure already
connects them: settling from A and settling from B bring back something in common, cells both ends reach. Then the
link only fills a gap between dots that were there (connecting the dots), and it sinks: the slow volume learns it, by
the core's own tie. If nothing is in common, the link stays in the fast volume, and when its test fails it evaporates
(its joint in the fast volume loosens, as in C82).

```
two volumes as C86 (slow: the core; fast: C82's loosening, fed by the slow one's winners)
each moment, an input of two things A and B:
    slow receives it                                   # recognition only: see "gate" below
    fast receives slow's winners                       # the fast volume ties A and B (a fast bond)
    if the fast volume recognised the moment whole     # the link has settled there: seen before, nothing new tied
        the sinking test, on the slow volume:
            brought(A) = cells the slow volume brings back from A alone
            brought(B) = cells it brings back from B alone
            fits when brought(A) and brought(B) share a cell
        fits:     the slow volume receives A with B and learns it      # sinks
        does not: in the fast volume, each cell of the moment loses one segment spanning A and B   # evaporates
```

**Given by hand, declared (CTR-2 CON-2):**
- **The gate.** The slow volume learns only when a link sinks. On every other moment its structure is put back as it
  was (from outside, through its cells' own grow and prune). How a volume gates its own learning is open (QST-17).
- **The sinking test** is run by the experiment: two settlings of a copy of the slow volume, compared. The question
  is whether the slow volume's own settling carries the signal, not how the machine would run the test.
- Settling goes to closure (the core, RM-4): nothing here limits how far it spreads.
