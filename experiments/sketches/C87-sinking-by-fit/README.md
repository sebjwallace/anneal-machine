# C87 · Sinking by fit

The owner's sinking and rising, on C86's two volumes: a link the fast volume has settled sinks into the slow one if
the slow structure already connects its two ends ([model.md](model.md)). Hypothesis, predictions, findings:
[hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt), [findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (under a second; `SEEDS=1` is the development seed).

**In short:** the slow volume's own settling separates links that connect its dots from links that don't, every time,
and gating on it keeps "cat flies" out. But the core's settling spreads to everything connected, so the dots are all
joined already: there is no near or far, "fish mammal" passes, and "bird walks" (true but new) can't get in. Settling
needs locality: heat lowering the bar.
