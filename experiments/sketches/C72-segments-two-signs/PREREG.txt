Sketch 39 pre-registration, written 2026-11-09 after smoke runs on development seeds 1-3 only, before any run on seeds 101-110.

Development (stated plainly).
 - The task brief asked for one dev seed; I used seed 1 for most smoke runs and seeds 1-3 for a diagnostic print (DIAG=1)
   and one 3-seed look at each mode. No setting was tuned on them; the constants below are sketch 36's or sketch 37's.
 - Found on the dev seed: sketch 36's world b "exception" "white dog" + "light off" -> "eating" IS the dog x light-off kind
   rule (Latin square row 1, column 3 = "eating"). So sketch 36's "exceptions 0.65" was one real exception (~0.3) plus one
   non-exception (~1.0). Here it is "calling", and the code now refuses any exception that equals the rule. Sketch 36 untouched.
 - Changed after the first dev look, before this file: NEV 6000 -> 9000; added "last quarter (prequential)" accuracies,
   because final probes of 2 individual exceptions per seed are single yes/no answers and too noisy to judge.

Frozen settings (sketch.js as of this file).
 Substrate, plain links, settling, readout, codes: sketch 36 unchanged (120 columns x 8 cells, K=6, cells + column units).
 Segments, both signs, one rule: SEGN=8 sampled uniformly from all active units, fire at THETA=0.75 of synapses active,
   drive +/- SEG_W*s*f (SEG_W=2), s0=0.3, fit s += 0.2(1-s), misfit s *= 0.5, removed below 0.1.
   Excitatory fit = its cell happened. Inhibitory fit = its cell did not happen.
   Excitatory growth (sketch 36): an outcome cell that happened but was not cast, with no excitatory segment firing for it.
   Inhibitory growth: a cell the automaton CAST (its own prediction) that did not happen, with no inhibitory segment of the
   same placement firing for it. Never from the correct answer, except in the nandug control.
 Placements:
   nand   - inhibitory segment on the cell: subtracts from the cell's drive.
   andnot - inhibitory sub-segment on one excitatory segment: subtracts from that segment's output only, floored at 0; a
            segment brought to 0 has not fired (it is neither credited nor blamed). Grown on every excitatory segment that
            fired for a wrongly cast cell and had no sub-segment fire. A wrong cell cast by plain links alone gets nothing.
            Sub-segments die with their segment.
   word   - sketch 37's single-word rule: every active unit -| a wrongly cast cell, w += 0.1(1-w); for a cell that happened
            and was not cast, every active unit's link onto it w -= 0.1. Inhibition = 2 * sum(w) / 12 (one token's full
            links = one full segment).
   exc    - excitatory segments only (sketch 36).
   nandug - control: nand with inhibitory segments grown on every outcome cell that did not happen (learnt from the answer).
 STOP (growth stop signal from heat), fixed now: every cell that is cast or happens updates a temperature
   T <- 0.9 T + 0.1 * misfit (misfit 1 if it happened and was not cast, or was cast and did not happen; T starts at 1).
   With STOP=1 a cell that qualifies for growth grows a segment of either sign only with probability T. Word links are not
   gated (they are not segments). STOP=0 is sketch 36's growth rule.
 NEV=9000 per seed, 12 blocks of 750, the switch at 4500. Test seeds 101-110.

Worlds (both): sketch 36's a and b, with "hiding" added to a's outcomes, plus exceptions:
   ind  - individual: a: "ginger cat"+"bowl full" -> "hiding", "white dog"+"door shut" -> "eating";
                      b: "ginger cat"+"bowl full" -> "hiding", "white dog"+"light off" -> "calling".
   ctx  - colour x context for every animal, no single word identifies it: "black" + "bowl empty" -> "leaving".
          The last animal never appears with it (probe: does it transfer?).
   end  - "brown bird"+"bowl full" -> a: "hiding", b: "eating"; until the switch, then the kind rule ("calling").
   late - from the switch on: a: "grey dog"+"bowl empty" -> "hiding"; b: "grey goat"+"door shut" -> "hiding".
   Held out: 3 rule combinations per animal, never touching an exception.

Runs (runall.sh): WORLD a and b x MODE exc, word, nand, andnot x STOP 0 and 1; MODE=nandug STOP=0 in both worlds;
   long runs NEV=18000 STOP=1 for nand and andnot in world b.

Primary measures: last-quarter prequential accuracy on ind / ctx / late exceptions and on rule events; final probes
(learning off) on seen rule combinations, held-out, neighbours of exceptions (sharing two of colour / animal / context),
far combinations; occurrences after the switch until the ended exception is right; segments alive and grown per block by
sign; synapse checks per event. "Pooled" = mean over the two worlds' 10-seed means.

Predictions (hypothesis 07, with numbers; my dev-seed expectation in brackets where it differs).
 P1 conjunctive exceptions: at STOP=0, nand and andnot reach ind >= 0.9 and ctx >= 0.9 (last quarter, pooled), and beat
    exc and word on ind by >= 0.2; seen rule accuracy no lower than exc's - 0.03.
    [dev: nand ~0.7-1.0 on ind, andnot ~0.6; ctx is easy for every mode (~0.85-1.0), so ctx will not separate them.
     I expect P1 to fail its 0.9 bar for andnot and possibly nand, but nand to beat exc clearly.]
 P2 no collateral: neighbours (each of the three kinds) for nand / andnot >= exc's - 0.03 and >= word's; far unchanged.
 P3 one rule: holds by construction (shared code and constants). It fails if either sign needed its own settings: none were
    tuned here, so this is judged by P1/P2 holding at the shared settings.
 P4 balance without a balance rule: inhibitory alive count in the last 4 blocks changes by < 10% of its final value
    (nand, andnot, both STOP). nandug grows >= 1.5x the inhibitory segments of nand and does worse on held-out.
 P5 growth settles: with STOP=1, growth in the last block < 25% of the first for both signs, and below STOP=0's last block;
    ind and seen rule accuracy within 0.05 of STOP=0. [dev: STOP=1 may slow exception learning - a cold cell rarely grows.]
 U  unlearning: the ended exception follows the rule again after <= 5 occurrences (median over seeds) for nand / andnot,
    and the final probe is >= 0.9.
 L  late exceptions (rule already hard): nand last quarter >= 0.8. [dev: andnot well below, ~0.35.]
 T  held-out transfer: nand / andnot within 0.1 of exc. [dev: nand drops ~0.2 below exc - soft inhibitory segments grown on
    neighbouring combinations fire on held-out ones. I expect T to fail for nand.]
 C  cost: nand >= 3x exc's synapse checks per event (every inhibitory segment is scanned); andnot <= 1.5x exc.
 Against (from the hypothesis): inhibitory segments do not beat word inhibition on ind; they hurt the rule or neighbours;
    growth of either sign never settles.
