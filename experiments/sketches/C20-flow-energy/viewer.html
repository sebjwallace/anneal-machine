Energy with the flow: C20, every rest move measured

As C19 (many copies, learn once, recognise anywhere), with one change to what counts. Total energy = structure (pixels stored + placements listed) + flow (the cost of explaining the last 100 scenes seen since the last rest with what exists now). Everything is priced in one currency taken from the world: a loose or wrong pixel costs 1, a stored pixel 1, a placement 1 plus the cost of naming which unit. At rest each candidate move (decompose, compose, and two new opposites: dissolve a composite back into its users, or fuse it into one plain part) is tried, the flow re-explained, and the move kept only if total energy fell. Rest is automatic every 98 flashes. 'Show a held-out pattern' shows two copies of an object never seen in training. Pattern noise starts at 0.
