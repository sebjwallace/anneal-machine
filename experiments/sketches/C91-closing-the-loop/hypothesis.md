# C91 · Closing the loop: hypothesis

Written 15 November 2026, after C90 confirmed that warm meets warm. The pieces so far were tested apart: two volumes
(C86), sinking by fit (C87), the lowering bar (C88), priming (C89, C90). Here they run as one cycle, the owner's picture
taken literally ([flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md), section 7): the surface turns, the deep
warms where it turns, a new piece settles at the surface, and it sinks only where it meets warm, welcoming ground.

**The rule (model.md):** once the surface has settled a new link, its fronts spread in the deep with the context's
warmth. Never meeting: it evaporates. Meeting on warm ground: it sinks, and the deep learns it. Meeting only on cold
ground: it stays up.

**Hypothesis:** a true link that fits sinks when the talk has warmed its place, and waits at the surface when it hasn't;
a link that fits nowhere evaporates; and the deep takes in fewer false links than if it sank everything that met, or
learnt everything.

**Expected and uncomfortable:** "fish mammal" fits by a far route (fish swim like whales, whales are mammals). After talk
of swimming, its place is warm, so it should sink: a plausible mistake, taken in because the context primed it.
