# C91 · Draft model: closing the loop (written before the code)

**For:** the whole cycle of [flow and heat](../../../docs/theories/flow-and-heat.md) section 7 in one machine: the
surface turns, the deep warms where it turns, a new piece settles at the surface, and it sinks only if it meets warm,
welcoming ground; otherwise it stays up, or evaporates if it fits nowhere.

```
two volumes as C86: the deep (C89's copy of the core, with warmth) and the surface (C82's loosening), the surface
fed by the deep's winners
an episode:
    context: a few moments through both volumes (the deep gated: it learns nothing from them)
        warm = the deep's winners in those moments: the poles that turned
    a new link A B arrives, twice: the first time the surface ties it; the second time the surface recognises it
        (settled at the surface)
    once settled, the meeting (C88's lowering bar, C89's warmth, from each end in the deep):
        never meet:                         evaporates: the surface loosens it (C87)
        meet, and the meeting touches warm: sinks: the deep receives A B and learns it, by its own tie
        meet only on cold ground:           stays up: still held at the surface, not learnt by the deep
    the episode ends: warmth goes (it does not fade gradually)
```

**The sinking rule is the owner's picture taken literally:** a warm piece meets a warm place and bonds. A piece that
fits but lands on cold ground is not refused; it stays at the surface, as conflicting or unconfirmed things do, until a
later moment warms the place it fits.

**Given by hand, declared:** the episodes (context and link); where an episode ends (warmth lasts until then); the deep's
gate; the meeting test run by the experiment, as C87-C90.
