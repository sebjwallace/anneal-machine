States and transitions

C23 and C24. A state is a settled scene, held as its "where" cells (one cell per thing, in that thing's column, at its offset from the middle). A transition links a state to the state that settles next. C23, exact memory: each cell that changed is linked to the cell it became; it only knows the exact places it has seen. C24, shared changes: at rest, transitions are compared at every shift, as parts are; when the whole change of one lines up with the whole change of another, what they share (the change, plus what stayed put around it) is kept without its position, so it can happen anywhere. The exact memory is used when it knows the step; shared changes otherwise. A cue is one cell of the next state; a context is held from elsewhere.
