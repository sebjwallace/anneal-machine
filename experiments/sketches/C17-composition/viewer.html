Composition at rest: C17, parts first, then layers of composites

Awake: each flash is explained by everything the machine has (parts and composites); if more than 10% is left unexplained or wrong, the flash is stored as a new memory. Rest, stage 1 (as C16): parts are compared and the hottest pair is factored into overlap and differences. Rest, stage 2: when the same group of units is listed in several memories (or composites), that group becomes a composite in the layer above, and everything containing the group uses the composite instead, if that lowers energy. Composites can contain composites, so layers build up. Energy = pixels stored + one for every unit listed. 'Rest now' shows each step. Under each column: how often it's used (and, for composites, how many units it's made of). Pattern noise starts at 0.
