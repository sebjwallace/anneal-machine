# C57 (on the C56 branch) · Whole days and replay at rest

Four runs: baseline (short walks, plain rest), whole days only, replay at rest only, both. Six pairs held back in every
run (no walk or day ever holds both); each asked with `think`, the answer checked for being real and by the store of
experiences. Outputs: out-c57-clear.txt (clear views while learning), out-c57.txt (1 of 5 pixels missing),
out-c57-learn-fb.txt (same, with context from above while learning).

| | clear views while learning | 1 of 5 missing while learning (about 2 % of views misread) |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 6 of 6 real, all trusted | 2 real, 4 undecided |
| whole days | 6 of 6 | 2 real, **4 made up and trusted** |
| replay at rest | 6 of 6 | **4 real**, 2 made up and trusted |
| both | 6 of 6 | 2 real, 4 made up and trusted |

**Whole days: no aeroplane.** The longest assembly stayed at 5 places even when days were 6–9 long, and joining took
as many rounds as before. Rest broke days back down into the shared stretches. Bigger assemblies didn't emerge from
learning here.

**Replay at rest: a small gain only when perception is imperfect** (4 real routes instead of 2). With clear views,
nothing to gain: everything already works.

**The real finding: misperceptions become memories.** With about 2 % of views misread while learning (the bridge read
as the hill, say), the misreading is stored in general memory *and* in the store of experiences as if it was lived.
So the experience check trusts a made-up step (junction → hill), because an "experience" says it happened. The check
is only as good as perception. Context from above while learning cut misreads only slightly (24 to 20).

**What this points to:** an experience seen once against many that disagree should count for less (how often a step
was lived, not just whether), and misfits noticed later should be able to correct what was stored. Engine (core)
unchanged; branch only.
