# C47 · One rule or two?

C46: checking a plan step by step against remembered walks catches made-up roads. But the energy rule that builds
general shapes also throws specific walks away. Question (owner): do remembered experiences need their own rule, the
way the hippocampus and cortex learn differently, or can one store under one rule do both?
- ONE RULE: only the energy-ruled memory; the walks it chose to keep are all there is to check against.
- TWO RULES: the same, plus a store of walks kept the moment they happen, refreshed when seen again, the longest-unseen
  fading when full (holds 50: a chosen number).
Measured as days go by, in three parks (small, 4×4 grid, 5×5 grid). Run: `PARK=small|grid|'grid 5x5' node run.js`.
