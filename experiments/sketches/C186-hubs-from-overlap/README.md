# C186 · Hubs from overlap

C185's anchored, widening search, with hubs formed over the kept ways of working: every pair compared, what they share
becoming a hub, and hubs compared in turn (hubs of hubs). Two kinds were tried. Hubs by relation (how one part sits
relative to another) used for completion: when one part moves, its related parts snap along by the most widely shared
relation, tried as one change. Hubs by position used to hold the shared core. **Relation hubs learned the second row in 3
of 3 seeds** (287 to 355 runs, 200 of 200). Position hubs, and no hubs, managed 1 of 3. A coincidence was settled on the way
in each relation seed and outgrown by the next example. Schema emerging from the overlap of experiences, relational
not positional, turns search into completion.
