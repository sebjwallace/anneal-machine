# C129 · Reading by bonds

Distillation D-03, the deepest step. Reading no longer looks anything up. The letters of a sentence arrive one a
moment. Each held chain runs on its own members' bonds and counts its own steps in order. A chain that completes
reads its state over those letters. Choosing between overlapping pieces by the cheapest description of the sentence,
this reads exactly as the index did on both books (energy within 2%). Choosing by the engine's own competition instead,
with no calculation at all, costs 0.3% to 5% in energy (*Alice*: words read whole 52% to 47%). The index was never
needed. What's left of reading to distil is the choice between overlapping pieces.
