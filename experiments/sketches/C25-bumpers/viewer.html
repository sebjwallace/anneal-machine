Imagining squares and diamonds

C25. The machine only ever sees pictures. First it learns the two shapes. Then it watches two-object demonstrations (every side, off-centre, moving or still) and, at rest, keeps as rules what the surroundings of each identical change share. Then it is given the first two pictures of a new start with several objects and imagines the rest on its own, with no input, drawing each step back onto the grid. Next to it, what really happens. The true rules: everything moves one cell a step; at a wall it turns round; when two come within one cell they both turn round, and the one hit from below flips (square ↔ diamond), the one hit from above becomes a square.
