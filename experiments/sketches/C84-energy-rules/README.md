# C84 · Energy rules at the level of cells: first candidates

A copy of the core with C82's loosening and four candidate rules for letting structure go (squeeze, used, yardstick,
fade), in a mixed world of recurring objects, one-off noise and a variable. The three shapes of heat are measured on
every moment. Hypothesis, predictions, findings: [hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md), [PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt),
[findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (about 7 minutes for five seeds; `SEEDS=1` for the development seed, whose output is `dev.txt`).
The core's tests on each arm: `ARM=core|squeeze|used|yardstick|fade node --test test/volume.test.js`.
