Staged growth upside down: C11, start big, then smaller layers

Staged growth in reverse: the first layer is big and specific (96 columns), and once it settles each new layer is smaller and more generic (48, 24, 12, 6). Columns start fully random (or, if you choose, as a faint haze) and sharpen as they learn (shade = strength). RM and RA: each new column starts as the average of a group of similar columns below; RM reads upwards (the specific match picks its parent), RA lets each layer compare all its columns. RF: fresh smaller layers. Restless idle columns (C12): a column that hasn't won for a while gets jiggled at random, more the longer it's idle and less the better it fits what it does win; the slider sets the most jiggle per flash and applies at once. Pattern noise starts at 0. The heat graphs use the most specific (first) layer; each layer's own heat is in the bottom graph. Use Skip 5,000.
