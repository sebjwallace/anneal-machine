# C86 · Poles and arms

The owner's poles and arms as two volumes of the same machinery: a slow one (the core) and a fast one (C82's loosening)
fed by the slow one's winners ([model.md](model.md)). Hypothesis, predictions, findings: [hypothesis.md](hypothesis.md),
[PREREG.txt](PREREG.txt), [findings.md](findings.md).

**Run:** `node run.js` (a few seconds; `SEEDS=1` is the development seed).

**In short:** each kind of knowledge survives in its own volume ("x" in the fast one 100%, "ABCD" in the slow one
100%), which one volume can't manage. But read together the two disagree, and the machine has no single answer: how
slow and fast settle against each other is the open question.
