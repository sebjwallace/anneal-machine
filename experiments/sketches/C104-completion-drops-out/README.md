# C104 · Completion drops out

The owner's idea: when an assembly completes, its activity drops out, and it leaves something behind that makes it
easier to complete next time (here: its gate needs one member fewer). Holding piles up: read as one text, four words
complete and stay held, and nothing read after them is recognised. Dropping out for one moment lets later words be
recognised, but completed words restart from their own chain and refractory members, so reading still ends held. One
member fewer straight after completing cancels dropping out.
