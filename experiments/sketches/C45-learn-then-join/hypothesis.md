# C45 · Learn, then join

C44 joined never-walked routes by pushing out from both ends, on memories handed to it. Here the memories are learnt
from experience by C43's learner (walks kept by energy, kinds with one or more slots). Park as C44 stage 2 (directed
paths, two ways from C to E, side paths, a part off on its own). Days: wandering walks of 3–5 places, one habit
(A→B→C→D→E) walked often, no walk holding both A and O. Every pair never walked together is asked: real route when
there is one, "no way through" when there isn't? Run: `WIN=100|300|1000 node run.js` (under 2 s).
