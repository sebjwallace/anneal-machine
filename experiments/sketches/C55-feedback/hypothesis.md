# C55 · Feedback from the abstract region to the visual region

Owner (after HTM): higher regions send context down. Kept open: no expectation of what it will do. Same two regions as
C54. Walking real routes, the abstract region sends down what it expects next (things at the next step in assemblies
holding the last two recognised; else the last one). It reaches the visual region in its own currency: naming an
expected thing costs log2 of the expected set, anything else log2 of all things. Measured with and without feedback in
every situation: expected views with 0–3 of 5 pixels missing, surprises (another landmark is there) with 0–2 missing,
and noise. Reading here is a simple single-object version of the engine's (best place per thing, saving against its
naming price, moving bar). Run: `node run.js` (4 s).
