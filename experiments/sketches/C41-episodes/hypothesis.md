# C41 · Episodes, minimal

Owner's distillation of the park: states are settled things; an episode is an assembly of states in time order, like a
scene is an assembly of parts in space. Two episodes, A → J → P and G → J → O, share one state (J). A and O were never
in one episode. Cued with A and O, do the two episodes come up and snap together at J, giving A → J → O?

No pixels: states are symbols and a state's "where" is its step in the episode. The engine's own cells and completion
code runs on it unchanged (as in the sandbox's symbol world). Episodes are given, not learnt (C38 showed walks are held
whole); this tests only recall and joining. Run: `node episodes.js` (0.2 s).
