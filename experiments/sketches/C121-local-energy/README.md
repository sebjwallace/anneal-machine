# C121 · Local energy

Does a minority register get drowned by the majority's prices? A stretch of the King James Bible is set inside
*Buster Brown*, learnt with C118's heat and energy, and the energy rule is judged either against all of memory
(global) or against a neighbourhood (the held patterns sharing material with the sentence being learnt). With global
energy the minority already learns well (450 patterns of its own, with `thee`, `thou`, `hath`, `unto`, `shall`, `god`
held), because a new register arrives unexplained: heat keeps it and learns from it. Local energy made both registers
worse (Buster Brown clean share 60% to 43%). The neighbourhood is too loose to pick out a register, so "local" just
meant a cheaper link everywhere, and scraps came back.
