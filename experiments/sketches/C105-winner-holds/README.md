# C105 · How a winner holds through a burst

On `l1a-reading`, L1B's winner is taken over by almost anything right after a burst, because a burst clears every
confirmation. Ten combinations compared: how confirmations last (cleared at a burst, kept for the winner, kept with
ties going to the challenger, or lasting 4 or 8 moments), and whether a burst projects up every member or only the one
picked. No rule holds through noise and changes when it should reliably: the best (a 4-moment window, picked only)
passes all four tests in 2 seeds of 10. Two problems underneath, neither the hold rule: a short assembly (`the_`) fully
confirmed can't be beaten by the phrase it is part of; and noise ending in a letter a learned word has ("purring",
"dog") brings that word's context through L1A.
