## The change

Reading by bonds already shows which held chains ran along a hot stretch as its letters arrived. A chain can run
along part of the sentence without having started at its first step: "matter" runs along "atter" in "hatter". The
engine copy now reports every such run of two or more steps. The partner for a hot stretch, and for each part of it
annealed after, is the held piece whose chain ran furthest within it; on a tie, the earliest formed (as the engine's
reuse prefers the earliest). No index, and no choosing among everything held: only what this reading brought up.

## Prediction

The same partners as the index, since both look for the held piece sharing the longest run. Energy and every reading
measure within 2% or 2 points of C130 on both books.

**Survives if** that holds.
