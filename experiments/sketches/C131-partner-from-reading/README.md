# C131 · Partner from reading

Distillation D-03, V5. The anneal partner comes from what reading brought up, the held chain that ran furthest along
the hot stretch, not from a three-letter index over memory. Taken with any run of two letters or more, the learning
got much worse (*Alice*: words read whole 51% to 24%). The reason: the old index never offered a partner sharing fewer
than three letters, a floor hidden in how it was built, and every result since C118 has rested on it. With the same
floor, the partner from reading matches the index on both books. The index is gone, but the floor is a new violation
(V13) still to remove. The step also showed that energy alone, as measured, can prefer learning less.
