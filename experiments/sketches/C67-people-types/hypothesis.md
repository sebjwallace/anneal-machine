# C67 · Types in a world of people, merged across frames

C66 again with people instead of heat (teachers, learners, cooks, diners; frames "the X teaches/guides the Y", "the X
feeds/serves the Y"), so the example doesn't borrow the engine's own words. Plus one step: C66's types were split
across frames, one group per verb. Here the groups are compared: groups sharing two or more members (the recall
rule's two) are one type. Question: do the four types come out whole and alone, from how few sentences? Givens in
run.js (G1-G4; G4, comparing the groups, is written by hand). Run: `node run.js` (out.txt).
