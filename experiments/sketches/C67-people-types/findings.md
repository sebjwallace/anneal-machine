## What it shows
- **Comparing the groups joins a type split across frames.** From 16 sentences, each seen once, the four types come
  out whole and alone, each built from the groups around both of its verbs. The same "two or more shared" rule that
  wakes a memory joins the groups.
- **People work as well as heat did.** Same shape of result as C66, without borrowing the engine's words.
- **It isn't steady below 16.** At 8 sentences all four types happen to come out; at 12, a short surrounding mixes a
  diner with a student and the merge carries the mix along (1/4). Merging spreads errors as well as types.
