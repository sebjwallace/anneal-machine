The funnel: C04, a shape you can change

Layers from detailed (top) to generic (bottom). Set the number of layers, the top layer's size, how much each layer keeps of the one above, and whether a layer's columns grow up to its size, are all there from the start (fixed), or grow freely; then Apply and restart. Each layer explains the flash with its own columns; a full layer can't add columns, so its winners stretch to cover what's left over. Cells link across all layers, so each flash forms one assembly from the specific to the generic. Noise can be steady or a wave (rising from 0 to the peak and back). The bar triples are held out.
